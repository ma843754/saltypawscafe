Our Story

How a love for dogs, coffee, and the coast became Satellite Beach's favorite café.

How It Started

Born From Salt Air & a Simple Question

Salty Paws Café grew out of a question every dog owner on the Space Coast has asked at least once: why isn't there a great coffee spot where my dog is actually welcome? In Satellite Beach, the answer used to be "there isn't one." So we decided to build it ourselves.

We opened on A1A with a short menu, a shaded patio, and a standing invitation for every pup in the neighborhood. What started as a small espresso bar quickly became the place locals come to start their day — sandy feet, wagging tails, and all.

Our Mission

A Welcome Mat for Everyone (Especially Dogs)

Our mission is straightforward: make Salty Paws the most welcoming spot on the coast for beachgoers, dog owners, and anyone who appreciates a well-made drink. That means a patio designed around dogs — with water stations, shade, and treats at the counter — and an indoor vibe that feels more like a friend's living room than a café.

Whether you're a surfer toweling off after a dawn session, a remote worker looking for good Wi-Fi and better coffee, or a family out for a Saturday stroll with the dog, you belong here. No dress code, no pretense, no leaving your best friend tied to a post outside.

What We Serve

Craft Espresso & Energy Drinks Worth Talking About

Coffee is the backbone of what we do. We source single-origin beans from a local roaster, dial in every shot by hand, and build our menu around drinks that actually taste like something — from our signature Salty Paws Latte with sea-salt foam to a 20-hour cold brew that's become a neighborhood staple.

On the energy side, we skip the canned stuff entirely. Every energy drink on the menu is hand-mixed in-house using real fruit, natural caffeine, and added electrolytes. Flavors like Tropical Storm and Watermelon Wave are made for Florida heat — refreshing, clean, and strong enough to fuel a full morning on the water.

Community

More Than a Café — A Gathering Place

Salty Paws exists because of the Satellite Beach community, and we try to give back at every opportunity. We partner with local animal rescues to host adoption events on the patio, feature work from area artists on our walls, and stock merchandise from fellow small businesses along the coast. If you're local, you'll see familiar faces here every morning. If you're visiting, you'll feel like a local before you finish your first cup.
