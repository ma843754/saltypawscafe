Our Story

How a love for dogs, coffee, and the beach became Salty Paws Café.

🌊

Born on the Beach

Salty Paws Café started with a simple idea: create a place where the beach community could gather with their dogs and enjoy a really good cup of coffee. Located in Satellite Beach, Florida, we opened our doors with a mission to blend craft beverages with the laid-back coastal lifestyle we all love.

Whether you're a surfer coming in from the waves, a dog parent on your morning walk, or just someone who loves a well-pulled espresso — this is your spot.

What We Stand For

Every decision we make circles back to community, quality, and the coastal way of life.

  • Locally roasted, ethically sourced coffee beans
  • A genuinely welcoming space for dogs of all sizes
  • Eco-friendly cups, lids, and packaging
  • Supporting local artists, surfers, and small businesses
  • Giving back to local animal rescues and shelters
