A Beach Café Built for You & Your Dog

Salty Paws Café is a laid-back coffee bar in the heart of Satellite Beach, Florida, where the dress code is flip-flops and every guest gets a tail wag at the door. We roast locally, pour carefully, and welcome dogs of every size onto our shaded patio.

Our menu is built around two things we do best: specialty espresso pulled from single-origin beans and hand-mixed energy drinks in bold, tropical flavors you won't find anywhere else on the coast. Pair either with an ocean breeze and you've got the perfect Satellite Beach morning.

What Makes Us Different

Most coffee shops ask you to leave your dog outside. We built ours so you'd never have to. Salty Paws is where sand-covered sneakers, wagging tails, and craft-quality drinks all belong at the same table — and that's by design, not accident.

🐶

Dog-Friendly Outdoor Seating

Our shaded patio was designed paws-first — with room to roam, fresh water stations, and treats at the counter. Leashed dogs of every breed and size are always welcome.

🌊

Beachfront, Relaxed Atmosphere

Set just steps from the sand in Satellite Beach, the café runs on salt air and good company. No rush, no pretense — just the sound of waves and the smell of fresh espresso.

☕

Espresso & Energy, Done Right

We pull every shot from locally roasted, single-origin beans and hand-mix our energy drinks in-house with real fruit and zero artificial junk. Quality you can taste in every sip.

🤝

Community-First Vibe

We're a neighborhood hangout for locals and a favorite stop for visitors. From adoption events with area rescues to showcasing local artists, Salty Paws puts community at the center of everything.
